About

The story behind the numbers.

Dishan started in freelance bookkeeping — reconciling ledgers and journal entries one client at a time — before stepping into structured audit practice at Gonsalves & Nayak Chartered Accountants. In just over a year he moved from Audit Executive to Senior Account Executive, a promotion earned by leading a six-person team through 70+ statutory, tax, and internal audits without missing a single reporting deadline.

What sets him apart isn't just technical accuracy, though that's a given — it's the speed at which he absorbs new systems. Dropped into an unfamiliar ERP with zero prior exposure, he was running compliance and reporting through it within weeks, and now trains other team members on it. He's equally comfortable finalizing a balance sheet, building a projected financial statement, or explaining a variance to a client who just wants a straight answer.

He's currently working through the CMA (US) program, layering US GAAP, internal controls, and management accounting on top of an audit background that's already grounded in real client work — not just theory.
